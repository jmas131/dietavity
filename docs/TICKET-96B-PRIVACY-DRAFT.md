# Ticket 96B privacy-notice draft

If enabled, Dietavity may process one food photograph, menu, nutrition label, supplement image, or short voice note that a customer voluntarily sends through WhatsApp. We use the minimum necessary information to provide general food guidance. Media is fetched temporarily from the communications provider, is not permanently stored by Dietavity, and may be sent to AI/transcription providers to create a short derived observation or transcript used in the conversation. Do not send medical records, prescriptions, identity documents, images of symptoms, or emergency information. Derived text is retained only as necessary for the conversation, security, and legal obligations. This wording requires Mexican privacy/legal review before activation.
